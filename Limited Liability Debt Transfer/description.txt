Rebalances corporate takeovers and stock trading with realistic corporate finance rules, expanding upon the "Buyout Debt" concept by Oralordos.

In vanilla Offworld Trading Company, buying out a rival completely erases 100% of their debt for free. This mod transfers outstanding debt to shareholders upon acquisition, while introducing true corporate limited liability and smart AI evaluation so buyouts remain fair, strategic, and balanced.

Key Features:
- Limited Liability Takeovers: Shareholders assume target debt upon buyout (10% per share owned), capped strictly by the tangible physical value of the structures, mines, and stockpiles acquired.
- Debt-Aware AI: Computer opponents factor attached debt liabilities and credit ratings into their financial planning, preventing the AI from committing chain-suicide buyouts on heavily indebted rivals.
- Transparent Stock Tooltips: Stock buy buttons clearly display the exact debt liability attached per share upon acquisition.
- Fair Stock Liquidation: Distressed, balance-sheet-insolvent shares sell for $0.00 rather than an artificial cash floor.

Based on "Buyout Debt" by Oralordos: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2051432580
