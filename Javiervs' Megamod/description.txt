This is a compilation of all my standalone OTC mods. You can find each of them individually in my steam workshop page.

Included Mods:
- Limited Liability Debt Transfer: Shareholders assume target debt upon buyout, capped strictly to physical assets acquired, with smart debt-aware AI and stock liability tooltips.
- Hold Price - Commodity Price Pegging: Peg any commodity's market price using automated buy and sell trades (SHIFT + CTRL + Click on the Buy button).
- UI+ Fixed: Comprehensive interface upgrade featuring projected building revenue, detailed tooltips, debt change rates, player list HUD improvements, and spectator tools.
- No Underground Nukes: Removes Underground Nukes from the regular Black Market shop, reserving them exclusively for rare sabotage auctions.
- Interdimensional No Resource Depletion: Disables randomized resource depletion on Interdimensional maps.
- Interdimensional Colony Classes: Unlocks 18 colony classes for skirmishes on Interdimensional maps.
