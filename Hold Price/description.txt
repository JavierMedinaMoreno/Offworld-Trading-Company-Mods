Hold Price allows you to peg any commodity's market price using automated buy and sell trades.

Key Features:
- Press SHIFT + CTRL + Click on the commodity Buy (+) button to toggle price pegging at the current market price.
- The commodity's stockpile numbers glow in persistent gold on your HUD whenever price pegging is active.
- Automatically purchases units if market prices drop below your peg, and sells excess inventory if prices rise above your peg.
- Manual Price Adjustments: Execute manual trades on the pegged commodity to seamlessly adjust your target price.
- Automatically adjusts trade size (from 10 to 5, 2, or 1 unit) to maintain maximum price stability.
- Disengages if you run out of cash to buy or run out of stockpile to sell.
