Updated and fixed version of the classic UI+ mod by Bug Kiooeht, restored for full compatibility with modern Offworld Trading Company releases.

Provides a comprehensive suite of quality-of-life interface enhancements, detailed tooltips, and spectator controls.

Key Features:
- Building Net Revenue: Displays projected net revenue on resource row hover build buttons to quickly identify profitable construction options.
- Enhanced Tooltips: Adds money-based color coding to cost tooltips and highlights terrain construction speed modifiers.
- Terrain Construction Preview: Shows terrain speed modifiers directly when placing buildings.
- Debt Rate of Change: Displays real-time debt increase/decrease rate beside your debt balance.
- Player List HUD Upgrades: Player cash + resources are always visible next to player names, and long names no longer get cut off.
- Camera Controls: Configurable maximum zoom-out distance and optional Zoom-to-Cursor mode.
- Hologram Selection: Cleanly reorganizes the Hologram choice interface.
- Spectator & Observer Tools: Caster Mode lobbies, spectator overlays, and advanced building status trackers next to player names.

Default Hotkeys:
- Hold J: View each player's debt next to their name.
- Ctrl+U: Copy map seed to clipboard.
- Ctrl+]: Toggle Observer advanced building status.
- Hold ]: Temporarily hide Observer advanced building status.
